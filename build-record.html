02 / Operator-controlled work

Internal desktop tool

Bespoke

More control over how information enters and leaves your workspace.

Read text, ask a question or have a live conversation through a compact desktop surface.

Implementation described here. No catalog concept is presented as a product capture.

Modes & implementation
  • speak: read text with playback controls.
  • ask: answer a prompt, then speak the response.
  • live: stream microphone audio and native audio replies, with interruption handling.

This describes the internal implementation. It does not establish a new end-to-end microphone or latency test, or a full accessibility screen reader.

Local implementation · Part of Bespoke

Point, highlight and act with operator controls. Checked steps can become learn-and-replay recipes.

Operator control & verified steps

03 / How the work holds together

Working locally

Baton

Traceable session handoffs, read receipts and a local fallback support coordination across agents.

The implementation choice

Handoffs leave a record of how work moved and whether it was received. Local fallback supports continuity across working environments.

Working locally

Blackboard

A readable activity and artifact record across people and agents.

The implementation choice

Plain JSON events keep the record readable even when the local viewer is down. The viewer surfaces handoffs, shared artifacts and fleet activity.

Portable context & capabilities

Packages carry useful context and capabilities between environments, with verification and recorded launches.

Scope & traceability

Package verification and launch records make what was loaded more inspectable. The work focuses on reusable context and capabilities across environments.

Internal reporting pipeline

Amazon Ads reporting

Source reports become queryable information through retrieval, durable storage and console-export ingestion.

Data path & dated evidence

Parquet snapshots feed a rebuildable DuckDB store and a read-only query interface. An additional path normalizes exported report columns and year-month periods.

Account data, client outputs and commercial performance are excluded.
